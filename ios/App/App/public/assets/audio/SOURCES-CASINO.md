# Card handling audio

Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl), Casino Audio 1.1.
License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Official pack: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio

WAV mirror: https://github.com/receptron/jinglescript/tree/main/samples/sfx
Mirror provenance: https://github.com/receptron/jinglescript/blob/main/samples/sfx/SOURCES.md

Included files:
- card-place-1.wav: Casino Audio card-place-2.ogg
- card-place-2.wav: Casino Audio card-place-3.ogg
- card-fan.wav: Casino Audio card-fan-1.ogg

The mirror decoded recordings to mono 48 kHz / 16-bit WAV, high-passed at
20 Hz, trimmed silence, and normalized to -1 dBFS. Playback is attenuated
in audio.js. No generated third-party sounds are included.

Button ticks and final chimes are synthesized locally by audio.js.
