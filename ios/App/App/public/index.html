THE VERY SHORT VERSION

Keep your head.
Avoid the bulls.

  1. Pick a card. Everyone chooses one from their hand. Cards turn over together.
  2. Find its row. Your card goes after the closest lower number. No lower number? Take a whole row.
  3. Five is the limit. Play the sixth card in a row and collect the first five bullheads. Your card starts a fresh row.
  4. Fewest bullheads wins. Cards ending in 5 have 2, cards ending in 0 have 3, matching doubles have 5, and 55 has 7 bullheads.

A hand lasts 10 turns. The game ends when someone reaches 66 bullheads.
